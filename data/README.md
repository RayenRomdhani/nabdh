# Data card: NABDH feeder benchmark v0.1

Real fault-labelled grid data at one-second resolution is not publicly available for
Tunisia, so this dataset is simulated, following the challenge instructions. Everything is
generated by `scripts/generate_dataset.py` from fixed seeds, so anyone can rebuild the exact
same files.

## What is simulated

One Tunisian low-voltage residential feeder: an MV/LV transformer feeding 24 houses along a
radial 230 V line. Eight houses have 3 kW rooftop PV.

| Signal | Model |
|---|---|
| Grid frequency | Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process around 50 Hz, standard deviation about 20 mHz, the order of magnitude seen on large synchronous grids |
| MV-side voltage | Ornstein-Uhlenbeck around 233 V (referred to LV), about 1 V standard deviation |
| Household load | base load, cycling air conditioner or heater, fridge, random kettle/oven bursts |
| PV | irradiance ramp for the time of day times a cloud factor shared by the neighbourhood |
| Voltage at each meter | radial voltage drop: 5 mΩ per cable segment, 40 mΩ service drop, 4 mΩ transformer |
| Measurement noise | 0.03 V, 0.2 % on current, 1 mHz on frequency |

Each meter and the transformer meter report voltage `v` (V), current `i` (A), active power
`p` (kW) and frequency `f` (Hz) every second.

## Episodes

| Episode | Split | Context |
|---|---|---|
| train_summer_evening | train (clean) | 18:30 to 19:30, AC cycling, PV fading |
| train_winter_morning | train (clean) | heaters, PV rising |
| test_summer_evening | test | as above, new seed |
| test_summer_heatwave | test | heavy AC, long compressor cycles |
| test_ramadan_iftar | test | synchronised cooking surge before sunset, dip during the meal |
| test_winter_morning | test | as above, new seed |

Training episodes contain no anomalies and are used only to calibrate thresholds and the
Isolation Forest. The Ramadan episode never appears in training, so it tests whether a
synchronised but legitimate load surge raises false alarms.

## Anomalies and events

Genuine events and faults are created inside the physics. Data-quality problems are injected
on the reported measurements only, after the physics is solved.

| Kind | Where | How it is generated |
|---|---|---|
| GRID_EVENT, freq_dip | physics | 180 mHz frequency drop over 4 s, exponential recovery (generator trip elsewhere on the grid) |
| GRID_EVENT, mv_sag | physics | MV-side voltage down 10 % for 20 s (upstream fault cleared) |
| EQUIPMENT_FAULT | physics | one house's service resistance rises from 0.04 to 0.5 Ω for 7 minutes, with random arcing spikes |
| STUCK | measurement | all channels repeat the value at the start |
| DRIFT | measurement | voltage offset ramps linearly to +3 to +8 V |
| BIAS | measurement | current and power scaled by 0.4 to 0.7 (CT error or tampering) |
| FABRICATED | measurement | synthetic voltage, power and a believable 50 Hz frequency wobble, generated without access to the grid |
| REPLAY | measurement | the meter's own genuine data from 10 to 30 minutes earlier |

Each test episode has 2 injections of every data-quality kind on random meters (4 to 10
minutes each), one frequency dip, one voltage sag and one equipment fault.
`ground_truth.csv` lists every one of them with meter, start, end and parameter
(drift size, bias factor or replay lag). Meter `-1` means feeder-wide.

## Labels for scoring

Detection runs on 60 s windows with a 30 s hop. A window takes an anomaly's label when the
anomaly covers at least half of it. Windows that touch an anomaly for less than half are
transitions and are left out of scoring. Any window that overlaps a grid event is labelled
GRID_EVENT.

## Files

- `<episode>_meters.csv.gz`: `t, meter, v, i, p, f`
- `<episode>_transformer.csv.gz`: `t, v, i, p, f`
- `ground_truth.csv`: injected anomalies and genuine events
- `episodes.json`: episode specifications and the train/test split

## Limits

The load and PV models are simple and the feeder is single-phase. Real Continental European
frequency recordings at 1 s exist publicly; replacing the simulated frequency with one of
them is planned for Phase 2.
